# 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy — Words Killer

生效日期 / Effective date: 2026-11-18

## 中文

Words Killer 无需注册或登录。开发者不通过本应用收集或接收个人数据、设备标识符、学习内容或使用分析；应用不包含广告、追踪或第三方分析 SDK。

### 本地存储

学习进度、收藏、自建及导入词条、复习事件保存在设备上的 SwiftData 数据库；语种、主题、学习目标及提醒偏好保存在 UserDefaults 中。应用没有开发者运营的服务器或应用内云同步服务。

### 语音与提醒

朗读使用 Apple 系统语音功能。已安装所需语音时可离线朗读，缺少语音时可能需要通过系统设置联网下载。复习提醒使用本地通知，仅在您授权后启用，不使用远程推送。

### 导入、导出与系统备份

应用仅在您选择文件或主动粘贴文本时读取相应内容。JSON 导出包含学习进度、收藏、自建词条和复习事件；学习偏好不包含在该导出中。导出位置由您选择，可能是 iCloud Drive 或其他文件服务，这些服务按各自政策处理数据。请妥善保管备份，不要在公开反馈中附上含个人内容的完整备份。

应用本地数据也可能随您启用的 Apple 设备备份保存；备份由系统和您的 Apple 账号设置管理。

### 删除与控制

「设置 → 清空学习进度与打卡日志」删除进度、收藏和复习事件，保留自建词库及学习偏好。自建词可在词库中删除。删除应用（而非「卸载 App / Offload App」）会移除设备上的应用数据；已导出文件及系统备份需在相应位置另行管理或删除。

### 联系与政策更新

隐私与使用问题请通过项目支持页面联系开发者：
https://github.com/jackplus/words_killer/issues

该页面为公开反馈渠道，请勿提交个人敏感信息。主动访问外部网站时适用该网站的隐私政策。政策如有调整，将更新本页及生效日期。

## English

Words Killer requires no account. The developer does not collect or receive personal data, device identifiers, study content or usage analytics through the app. There are no advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDKs.

Progress, favorites, custom/imported words and review events are stored locally using SwiftData. Preferences are stored in UserDefaults. There is no developer-operated server or in-app cloud sync.

Speech uses Apple system voices and works offline when the required voices are installed. Downloading missing voices in system settings may require internet access. Optional study reminders use local notifications with your permission, not remote push notifications.

Files and pasted text are read only when you choose to import or paste them. JSON exports contain progress, favorites, custom words and review events, but not study preferences. You choose the destination, which may be iCloud Drive or another file provider subject to its own privacy policy. Keep exports private. Apple device backups may also include local app data according to your system and Apple account settings.

Resetting study progress in Settings deletes progress, favorites and review events while retaining custom words and preferences. Custom words can be deleted from the wordbook. Deleting the app (rather than offloading it) removes its on-device data. Manage or delete exported files and system backups separately.

For support or privacy questions, contact the developer through the public project support page at https://github.com/jackplus/words_killer/issues. Do not post sensitive information or complete personal backups. External websites apply their own policies. Changes to this policy will update this page and its effective date.

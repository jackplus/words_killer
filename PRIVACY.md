# 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy — Words Killer

**生效日期 / Effective Date:** 2026-11-16

## 中文说明

**Words Killer** 是一款纯本地离线运行的背单词应用。我们尊重并保护您的隐私：

1. **不收集个人数据**：本应用无需注册或登录，不会收集、上传、存储或出售您的姓名、邮箱、设备标识符（IDFA）、位置信息或任何个人身份数据。
2. **纯本地学习记录**：您的学习设置（如目标语言、难度偏好）与背词记忆进度（掌握状态、复习次数与时间间隔）仅通过 Apple 原生 `UserDefaults` 与 `SwiftData` 保存在您的设备本地存储中。
3. **无第三方分析或广告 SDK**：本应用未集成任何第三方统计、追踪、崩溃上报或广告框架。
4. **语音朗读**：单词与例句朗读完全调用 iOS 系统内置的 `AVSpeechSynthesizer` 在设备端完成，不会向外部服务器发送语音或文本请求。
5. **数据删除**：卸载本应用或在系统设置中清除应用数据，即可彻底删除所有本地学习记录。

---

## English

**Words Killer** is an offline-first vocabulary flashcard application. Your privacy is respected by design:

1. **No Personal Data Collection**: The app requires no account or login and does not collect, transmit, store, or sell your name, email, device identifiers, location, or any personally identifiable information.
2. **Local-Only Storage**: Your preferences (target language, difficulty level) and spaced-repetition study progress are stored exclusively on your device using Apple's native `UserDefaults` and `SwiftData`.
3. **No Third-Party SDKs or Tracking**: The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking frameworks.
4. **On-Device Speech**: Word and sentence pronunciation uses iOS's built-in `AVSpeechSynthesizer` locally on your device without sending text or audio data to external servers.
5. **Data Deletion**: You can delete all stored study data at any time by uninstalling the app from your device.
